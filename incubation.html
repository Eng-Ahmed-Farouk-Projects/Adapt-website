WHY INCUBATION MATTERS

Good projects need more than a good idea.

Many teenagers develop promising ideas during workshops, competitions, or collaborative activities. Without guidance, structure, resources, or a supportive community, those ideas can disappear when the event ends.

Projecta aims to bridge that gap. Teams can refine their concepts, understand the problem they are solving, develop their products, gather feedback, strengthen their models, and explore opportunities for implementation.

Incubation is a developmental journey — not simply a workspace or a request to complete a business plan.

THE PROJECTA DEVELOPMENT PATH

Eight stages of
structured progress.

Each stage helps a team understand what it is building, why it matters, who needs it, and how it can become more useful and sustainable.

01

Application & Selection

Review the idea, objectives, potential impact, feasibility, innovation, and commitment of the team.

02

Problem Validation

Investigate the problem, understand intended users, and test whether the proposed solution addresses a real need.

03

Business & Project Development

Clarify the value proposition, business model, target audience, development strategy, and project objectives.

04

Technical & Creative Development

Develop prototypes, software, physical models, designs, or early versions of the proposed solution.

05

Mentorship & Capacity Building

Strengthen technical, entrepreneurial, organizational, communication, and teamwork capabilities.

06

Testing & Improvement

Gather feedback, test the solution, identify weaknesses, and iterate based on what is learned.

07

Pitching & Presentation

Communicate the project's value, demonstrate progress, explain the model, and present its potential.

08

Launch & Growth Exploration

Explore ways to introduce the project to its intended audience, establish collaborations, or continue venture development.
