PROJECT-TA HACKATHON

Not just a coding competition.

Technology is one ingredient in innovation — not the whole recipe. Participants can combine technical knowledge, design, business thinking, research, creativity, and presentation skills to address a challenge from different angles.

The goal is to help teams progress from problem discovery to a tangible concept, prototype, business model, or technical implementation. Mentorship and feedback support that journey.

THE HACKATHON EXPERIENCE

Eight stages.
One shared challenge.

Each stage gives teams a clearer understanding of the problem, the people affected, and the value of the solution they are creating.

01
DISCOVER

Challenge discovery

Participants are introduced to challenges and explore problems that affect people, communities, industries, or the environment.

02
FORM

Team formation

Interdisciplinary teams come together, recognize strengths, and identify the responsibilities required to move forward.

02 / CONNECT
03
INVESTIGATE

Research & ideation

Teams research the chosen problem, understand target users, brainstorm possible responses, and evaluate ideas.

03 / EXPLORE
04
DEVELOP

Solution development

Teams begin shaping prototypes, concepts, business models, designs, or technical implementations.

04 / BUILD
05
REFINE

Mentorship & guidance

Teams receive guidance and feedback during development to challenge assumptions and strengthen their work.

05 / IMPROVE
06
COMMUNICATE

Final presentation

Participants explain the problem, demonstrate their work, and communicate why the solution matters.

06 / PRESENT
07
EVALUATE

Evaluation & recognition

Projects can be assessed against innovation, feasibility, impact, technical implementation, creativity, and presentation.

07 / LEARN
08
CONTINUE

Further development

Promising projects can explore mentorship, incubation, collaboration, and the next stage of implementation beyond the event.
