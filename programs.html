02 / ADAPT MEDIA LAB

Creative talents.
Professional storytellers.

A practical pathway for developing the creative and technical abilities required to produce professional visual and digital content.

From camera to final cut.

Participants explore photography, camera settings, composition, lighting, visual storytelling, scriptwriting, storyboarding, shot planning, camera operation, cinematography, interviews, documentary storytelling, event coverage, and short-form content.

Editing and post-production can include Adobe Premiere Pro, Adobe After Effects, motion graphics, visual effects fundamentals, color correction, sound design, compositing, and animated titles.

Graphic design and digital media cover typography, color theory, visual hierarchy, layouts, social graphics, posters, brand identity, content strategy, campaign design, and audience engagement.
