02
FROM OPPORTUNITY TO VENTURE

Entrepreneurship

Entrepreneurship gives young people a practical way to recognize problems, create value, develop solutions, and explore sustainable initiatives.

Idea development

  • Problem identification and opportunity recognition
  • Brainstorming and design thinking
  • Preliminary market research and customer understanding
  • Feasibility evaluation and solution development

Business development

  • Business Model Canvas and value propositions
  • Customer segments, revenue, and cost structures
  • Competitor analysis and market positioning
  • Pricing, MVPs, prototyping, testing, and validation

Venture development

  • Branding, marketing, and customer feedback
  • Pitch development and presentations
  • Founding teams, delegation, and decision-making
  • Risk assessment, resilience, and adaptation

Social entrepreneurship is included: projects can address environmental, educational, social, and community challenges, not only financial goals.

03
SKILLS INTO PROFESSIONAL SERVICES

Freelancing & Professional Readiness

Freelancing is treated as a full professional-development pathway. A skill becomes marketable through a clear service, evidence of capability, professional communication, and reliable delivery.

Professional identity

  • Personal branding and portfolio structure
  • Project documentation and professional profiles
  • Demonstrating technical and creative work
  • Understanding client expectations and ethics

Client work

  • Finding clients and understanding requirements
  • Writing proposals and negotiating scope
  • Pricing services and managing deadlines
  • Handling revisions and delivering quality work

Independent practice

  • Service packaging and basic financial planning
  • Time management and productivity
  • Customer satisfaction and long-term relationships
  • Web, design, editing, writing, data, and digital services

Learning pathways respect applicable age, platform, contractual, and labor requirements.
